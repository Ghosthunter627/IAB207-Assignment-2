Riverside Night Bites

Spend the evening by the river with some of Brisbane's best street-food vendors, local desserts and live entertainment in the heart of South Brisbane.

Date Saturday, 12 September 2026
Time 5:00 PM - 10:30 PM
Location South Bank Cultural Forecourt
Suburb South Brisbane

About the event

An evening built around Brisbane food

Riverside Night Bites brings together a curated selection of local food stalls, street-food vendors and dessert makers for an after-dark dining experience along the Brisbane River.

Guests can explore a range of cuisines throughout the evening, with vendors serving dishes inspired by Asian, Mediterranean, Australian and Latin American street food. The event also includes live acoustic entertainment, communal seating and riverfront dining areas.

Entry tickets provide access to the event precinct. Food and beverages are purchased directly from individual vendors unless otherwise stated.
